About Briefly AI

Briefly AI is a text summarization application designed to reduce long text into concise, meaningful summaries. It uses a pretrained BART model when available and switches to an extractive summarization algorithm when the model is unavailable.

How the Fallback Works

The extractive summarizer selects important sentences from the original text rather than generating new sentences.
